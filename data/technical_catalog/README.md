# LAT-CES Technical Product Research Catalog

This catalog is a **read-only engineering input source** populated from official manufacturer sources.

It is intentionally broader than building materials. It can contain technical products used by any LAT-CES module, including:

- fans and air-handling equipment;
- heat pumps and HVAC equipment;
- ducts, pipes, fittings, valves and grilles;
- pumps and hydronic equipment;
- electrical equipment, cables and luminaires;
- structural products and construction systems;
- insulation, membranes, boards, finishes and other building products;
- future module-specific equipment.

## AI boundary

The AI research layer may:

1. locate official manufacturer pages and technical documents;
2. extract manufacturer-declared technical characteristics;
3. preserve units, product identity, source URL, document identity and retrieval date;
4. flag missing or ambiguous manufacturer data.

The AI research layer must **not**:

- choose a product for a project;
- declare code compliance;
- determine design values;
- calculate safety factors or load combinations;
- approve a material/product for engineering use;
- infer unstated performance values.

Engineering modules read this catalog and independently determine applicability, design values, code checks and project decisions.

The catalog is informational and traceable, not an engineering approval database.
