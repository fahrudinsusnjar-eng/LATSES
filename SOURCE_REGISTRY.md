# TRAMP — Official Source Registry

TRAMP does not treat an input claim as a fact.

Every decisive answer must be traceable to an identified source and, where applicable:
- institution
- jurisdiction
- source type
- authority level
- URL
- publication/effective date
- verification timestamp
- version/status

Priority classes:
1. Law / regulation / official gazette
2. Government ministry / agency
3. EU / international institution
4. Official statistics
5. Research / technical evidence
6. Secondary reporting — contextual only

Core source domains to integrate:
EU institutions, Government of Bosnia and Herzegovina and entity institutions, FAO, IFAD, World Bank, EBRD, EIB, WHO, IOM, UN agencies, GEF, GCF and official national statistics.

No live source is simulated. If a source cannot be verified, TRAMP must mark the information as unverified.
